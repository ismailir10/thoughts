# Project instructions

## Scope

This folder contains the shareable **Seruput Entertainment goes to Karuizawa** trip plan.

## Locked trip requirements

- Dates: 21–23 September 2026.
- Accommodation: Ridge Garage Lodge, two sites, both nights.
- Quoted accommodation amount: ¥132,000 total as supplied; verify the final reservation screen before payment.
- Target roster: eight people, four men and four women.
- All participants are Indonesian. Do not add Chindo details to the written context.
- Transport: highway bus between Tokyo and Karuizawa, plus local bus between Karuizawa Station and the Rising Field area.
- Prioritize activities around Rising Field and Ridge Garage Lodge facilities.
- Keep Muslim-friendly food requirements visible and verify ingredients rather than assuming halal certification.
- Wednesday’s normal return target is the 16:30 highway bus; 17:30 is the latest planned backup.

## Editing rules

- Keep `itinerary.md` as the primary itinerary and update `README.md` when its assumptions or workflow change.
- Keep `karuizawa-event-poster.png` at the top of the itinerary unless the user requests a replacement.
- Preserve participant names, starting areas, dates, accommodation, transport, and cost assumptions unless the user changes them.
- Use a real Google Maps destination link for every destination point; do not use generic map-search placeholders when a direct pin is available.
- Include a real image URL for each day’s highlight and both Ridge Garage Lodge visuals, with captions where source attribution is needed.
- Keep every day in the `Time | Sunny plan | Rain plan` format.
- Label live prices, schedules, availability, and facility details as estimates until rechecked.
- Keep waterfall visits optional when weather, safety, or bus timing is unsuitable.
- Check Markdown consistency, arithmetic, image presence, and the Wednesday arrival cutoff before handoff.

## Repository workflow

- Every intentional change must be reviewed, committed, and pushed to the project repository when credentials and network access are available.
- Before pushing, review the diff, run `git diff --check`, and confirm that no unrelated files are included.
- Record the resulting commit or any deployment blocker in the final response.
