## Day plans

### Monday 21 September · Tokyo → Ridge Garage Lodge

![Monday highlight: arriving at Karuizawa Station](https://karuizawa-kankokyokai.jp/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/3309748_s.jpg)

*Highlight: arrive through Karuizawa Station, then connect to Rising Field. [Image source: Karuizawa Tourism Association](https://karuizawa-kankokyokai.jp/course/63848/).*

- Take the highway bus from [Shibuya Mark City](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Shibuya+Mark+City+bus+terminal) to [Karuizawa Station](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Karuizawa+Station). Current reference fare: **¥3,300 one way**; confirm the live fare and timetable when booking.
- Buy or carry lunch and simple groceries that can travel by bus.
- Take the local bus from [Karuizawa Station](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Karuizawa+Station) to [Rising Field Karuizawa](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Rising+Field+Karuizawa). Current reference fare: **¥460 one way**.
- Check in, assign the two sites, inspect sleeping and cooking arrangements, and locate bathrooms, water, charging, waste disposal, and emergency contacts.
- Walk around the established paths, use the available lodge/common/outdoor facilities, cook dinner, and leave time for prayer and rest. Stargazing is optional if weather and quiet-hour rules allow it.

### Tuesday 22 September · Rising Field day

![Tuesday highlight: Rising Field forest accommodation](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/019307_17128fd8407d428a9cdac4bc7af8881f~mv2.png/v1/fill/w_980%2Ch_661%2Cal_c%2Cq_90%2Cusm_0.66_1.00_0.01%2Cenc_avif%2Cquality_auto/Mask%20group%20%2844%29.png)

*Highlight: spend the main day in Rising Field’s forest setting. [Image source: Rising Field official camping page](https://www.rising-field.com/rfk-camp).*

- Breakfast, warm-up, and an easy nature walk or photo challenge on established paths.
- Optional **8–12 km run** for interested participants; others can take a shorter walk or stay at the lodge.
- Return for lunch, hydration, prayer, rest, and facility time. Keep one site quiet and the other social if the layout permits.
- Use the outdoor area for light games, group photos, journaling, and a shared reflection. Add a nearby Rising Field activity only if it is open and suitable.
- Cook dinner, clean the sites, and gather for non-alcoholic drinks, conversation, prayer, and games. A campfire is conditional on lodge permission and safe cleanup.

### Wednesday 23 September · Return to Tokyo

![Wednesday highlight: optional Shiraito Falls nature stop](https://www.legrand-karuizawa.jp/photo/img/170613/main_sp_img_001.jpg)

## Food and practical requirements

Everyone should confirm food requirements before grocery shopping. Japanese food can contain pork stock, lard, mirin, cooking sake, alcohol, gelatin, dashi, or other non-halal ingredients.

`ムスリムです。豚肉、ラード、酒、みりんを使わない料理をお願いします。`

Plan around clearly labelled groceries, vegetables, fruit, tofu, seafood, and halal pantry basics from Tokyo. Confirm lodge cookware, grills, and shared utensils before cooking.
