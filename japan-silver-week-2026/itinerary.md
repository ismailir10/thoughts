## Monday 21 September: Tokyo to the lodge

![Monday highlight: arriving at Karuizawa Station](https://karuizawa-kankokyokai.jp/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/3309748_s.jpg)

*Monday highlight: arrive through Karuizawa Station, then connect by local bus to Rising Field. [Image source: Karuizawa Tourism Association](https://karuizawa-kankokyokai.jp/course/63848/).*

### Morning: highway bus to Karuizawa

1. Take a highway bus from [Shibuya Mark City bus terminal](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Shibuya+Mark+City+bus+terminal) to [Karuizawa Station](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Karuizawa+Station).
2. Use the [Highwaybus.com Tokyo/Shibuya–Karuizawa route](https://www.highwaybus.com/gp/inbound/inbRouteList?arefNameCd=110&rrefNameCd=113) to select the exact departure and reserve seats together.
3. Budget **¥3,300 per person each way** as the current reference fare; check the booking screen because fares and availability can change.
4. Buy or carry lunch and groceries in Tokyo or around Karuizawa Station. Keep the shared food plan simple enough to transport by bus.

### Afternoon: Karuizawa Station to Rising Field

- Take the local bus from [Karuizawa Station](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Karuizawa+Station) to [Rising Field Karuizawa](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Rising+Field+Karuizawa). Reference fare: **¥460 one-way**.
- Walk from the stop to Ridge Garage Lodge, check in, assign the two sites, and confirm the facility rules.
- Settle luggage, inspect the sleeping and cooking arrangements, and identify bathrooms, showers, water points, charging points, waste disposal, and emergency contacts.

### First lodge-area program

- Eat a packed lunch or prepare a quick shared meal.
- Take a relaxed orientation walk around the [Rising Field area](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Rising+Field+Karuizawa), staying on established paths and respecting other guests.
- Use the lodge’s available common and outdoor facilities for group conversation, simple games, photography, and rest.
- Prepare dinner together, leaving time for prayer and an early quiet period after the bus journey.
- If the weather is clear, finish with a short stargazing or night-sounds session from the lodge area; follow lighting, fire, and quiet-hour rules.

This is the main activity day. Keep the group near the lodge so the trip benefits from the two-night booking instead of spending the day on transfers.

### Morning: nature and movement

- Breakfast at the lodge.
- Group warm-up and an easy walk through the nearby natural area.
- Photo challenge or nature observation: trees, streams, insects, birds, and landscape details. Do not pick plants or leave the established paths.
- Optional 8–12 km run for participants who want it, using safe marked roads and paths after checking the weather and local conditions. The rest of the group can continue the shorter nature walk or use the lodge facilities.

### Midday: shared meal and facility time

- Return to the lodge for lunch, hydration, prayer, and rest.
- Use the facilities that are included in the reservation: common area, cooking area, power, showers, outdoor space, and any listed lodge amenities.
- Run a low-equipment group program such as team games, card games, sketching, journaling, or a photo review.
- Keep one site available as a quiet/rest space and the other as the social/cooking space, if the lodge layout allows.

### Afternoon: maximize the area

- Take a second short walk or choose a nearby Rising Field activity that is open and appropriate for the group.
- Use the outdoor area for group photos, light recreation, and a shared reflection session.
- Return before dusk to cook dinner, clean the cooking area, and prepare warm layers and lights for the evening.

### Evening

- Shared dinner and non-alcoholic drinks.
- Group discussion, prayer, games, and quiet conversation around the lodge.
- Campfire or fire-based activity only if it is explicitly allowed by Ridge Garage Lodge and the group follows its fuel, supervision, and cleanup rules.
- Finish with a clear lights-out time so everyone can rest.

## Food and Muslim-friendly checklist

Japanese dishes can contain pork stock, lard, mirin, cooking sake, alcohol, gelatin, or non-halal meat. Use this request when buying food or eating out:

`ムスリムです。豚肉、ラード、酒、みりんを使わない料理をお願いします。`

Prioritize verified vegetarian dishes, seafood, tofu, vegetables, fruit, and packaged groceries with clear labels. Carry halal pantry basics from Tokyo for the lodge. Confirm whether lodge cookware, grills, and shared utensils can be used for the group’s food requirements.
